what is estimated?

Your room measurements set the scale. The 119 in wall is modelled as 118 in so it closes against the 133 in overall depth.

The window, wall curve and fittings are photo estimates. The booth dimensions are a proposed fit. Walking uses a 40 cm body width; real shoulder clearance varies.

The two players and mixer use the published CDJ-3000X and DJM-V10-LF dimensions. Two 25 mm gaps give a total width of 117.68 cm.

The room is a working mockup, not a measured site survey.
